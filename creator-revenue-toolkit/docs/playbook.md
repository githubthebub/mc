# Creator Revenue Playbook

A condensed, practical reference behind the numbers in the toolkit. Three levers:
**earn more, spend less, expand.** Work them in that order — pricing fixes pay off
in days, cost fixes in weeks, expansion in months.

---

## 1. Earn more

### Price sponsorships from benchmarks, not from fear

Sponsored-content pricing is CPM-shaped: your average reach × a per-1,000 rate,
adjusted for engagement. Rough 2025–26 host-read / integrated ranges (USD):

| Platform | Integrated placement | Dedicated placement | Priced against |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube | $20–$35 CPM | $45–$70 CPM | average views per video |
| TikTok | $10–$20 CPM | $20–$35 CPM | average views per video |
| Instagram | $8–$15 CPM | $18–$30 CPM | average reel/post reach |
| Podcast | $18–$25 CPM (30s) | $25–$40 CPM (60s mid-roll) | downloads per episode (30-day) |
| Newsletter | $25–$40 CPM | $45–$65 CPM (dedicated send) | opens per send |
| Twitch | $8–$15 / avg viewer | $20–$35 / avg viewer | average concurrent viewers |

Adjustments that are standard practice — charge for them:

- **Usage rights** (brand runs your content as a paid ad): +25% or more, time-boxed.
  Perpetual usage should cost multiples of the base rate.
- **Category exclusivity** (you won't take a competitor for N days): +15–25%.
- **Rush delivery**: +15%.
- **High engagement**: if your engagement rate beats the platform norm
  (YouTube ~4%, Instagram ~2%, TikTok ~5%), your audience is worth more per view —
  quote above the midpoint.

Negotiation mechanics that reliably raise the closed price:

1. Ask for the campaign budget before quoting.
2. Anchor at the top of your range; expect to settle 10–20% below your anchor.
3. Sell packages (3-video series at a 10–15% discount) — higher LTV, less churn.
4. Re-quote every returning sponsor annually. If nobody ever pushes back on your
   rate, it is too low.

### Fix the streams you already have

- **Ads**: RPM follows watch time and advertiser-friendly topics; longer videos
  with mid-rolls materially outearn shorts on a per-view basis.
- **Affiliate**: move links above the fold in descriptions, add spoken CTAs, and
  prefer direct partner programs (10–30%) over marketplace defaults (1–4%).
- **Memberships**: 3 tiers, cheapest tier ≥ $5, and give the middle tier the best
  value-per-dollar — that's where most members land.

## 2. Spend less

Healthy creator businesses run **20–40% of revenue as costs**. Above 40%,
audit before you grow — scaling an unprofitable pipeline just scales the loss.

The four cuts that recover the most, fastest:

1. **Consolidate overlapping subscriptions.** Two music libraries, two stock-asset
   plans, three AI tools. Keep the one you'd repurchase today; cancel the rest.
2. **Switch keepers to annual billing.** Typical saving is 15–20% per tool.
3. **Cancel-and-see.** Anything not marked *essential* (publishing stops without it)
   gets cancelled for a month. Re-subscribe only if it was actually missed.
4. **Renegotiate your biggest line item.** For most creators that's editing.
   A per-video flat rate usually beats hourly once volume is steady; a retainer
   beats per-video at 4+ videos/month.

Spend that *is* usually worth it: an editor (converts your hours into volume),
decent audio, and anything that removes a step between "recorded" and "published".

## 3. Expand

### Repurpose before you produce

New platforms should start as **distribution of existing work**, not new production:

| You make | It becomes |
|---|---|
| One long YouTube video | 3–5 shorts/reels/TikToks, a newsletter section, a podcast episode |
| One podcast episode | Audiogram clips, quote graphics, a blog/SEO post |
| One newsletter | Thread/carousel, script outline for video |

Only add net-new production when a repurposed format proves demand.

### Own the relationship

Platform audiences are rented; an email list and a community are owned. Push every
platform toward at least one owned channel — it's the only asset an algorithm
change can't touch, and it's what makes product launches work.

### Ladder of expansion (in order of typical ROI)

1. **Repurposing** to 1–2 adjacent platforms (near-zero marginal cost).
2. **Email list** (owned distribution; compounds forever).
3. **First digital product** — build the thing your comments/DMs already ask for.
4. **First hire: an editor** — buys back the hours every other stream needs.
5. **Recurring community/membership** — stable floor under lumpy sponsor income.
6. **Premium offers** (course, cohort, consulting) once trust is deep.

### Diversification target

The toolkit scores diversification by *effective number of streams*
(1 / Herfindahl index of revenue shares). Rules of thumb:

- No single stream > 50% of revenue.
- 3–4 *effective* streams ≈ resilient (score 75–100).
- Recurring revenue (memberships + products) covering your fixed costs is the
  point where the business stops being fragile.
