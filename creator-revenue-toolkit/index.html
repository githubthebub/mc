What should you charge sponsors?

Rates are computed from your reach and engagement against 2025–26 industry CPM benchmarks. They are a starting point for negotiation — never a ceiling.

Negotiation checklist

  • Quote the range, anchor high. Open at the top of your computed range; brands expect to negotiate down 10–20%.
  • Charge separately for usage rights. A post on your channel and an ad the brand runs with your face are two different products.
  • Bundle into packages. A 3-video series at a 10–15% discount locks in predictable revenue and beats three one-off negotiations.
  • Ask for the brief’s budget first. “What budget did you have in mind for this campaign?” regularly surfaces numbers above your rate card.
  • Never work for exposure alone. Gifted product can supplement — not replace — a fee once you have real reach.

Expansion playbook

  • Repurpose before you produce. One long video becomes 3–5 shorts, a newsletter section, and a podcast segment. New platforms should start as distribution, not new production load.
  • Own the relationship. An email list or community you control is the only audience an algorithm can’t take away. Push every platform toward it.
  • Productize what people already ask you. The questions in your comments and DMs are a free market survey for your first digital product.
  • Hire for your bottleneck, not your job title. The first hire is usually an editor — it converts your hours into more publishing volume, the input every revenue stream shares.
  • Raise prices annually. If 100% of sponsors accept your rate without pushback, the rate is too low.
