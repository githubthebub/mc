## Decide
- captions: key words only (three or fewer), with the accent word index. Not filler.
- dropouts: the punchlines, takeaways and key lines where the music should hard-cut.
- fadeouts: sentences that end a section (the music fades over the ~20 s before).
- swells: payoff sentences the music should build into.
- zooms: short word ranges that deserve an abrupt emphasis zoom (1.2-1.4).
- sfx: risers that END on a reveal (only when something important follows), hits ON a word,
  whooshes/pops/shutters only where a graphic appears.
- notes: judgment calls and anything that needs the creator's eyes.
