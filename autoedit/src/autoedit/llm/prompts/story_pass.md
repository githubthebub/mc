## What to decide
1. experience_fit: what these viewers come for and what that means for this edit.
2. story_pass: for each section: time, what happens, green (reason to care) or purple (payoff),
   conflict or none, mood, litmus gaps, action (keep, cut, montage, voiceover, reorder, beat, music move).
3. hook: which sentences open the video so the most extraordinary thing is shown first (proof, not
   promise), whether they must be moved to the front, whether the premise is restated.
4. litmus_gaps: voiceover lines that fill clarity gaps, each after a specific sentence.
5. cut_sentences: tangents, repeats, bad takes, dead stretches.
6. montages: ranges to compress, with a target length.
7. beats: "shut up and show it" pauses after key phrases (word id), with a 1-3 word caption fill
   when a visual would help, and longer breaks after emotional peaks.
8. protect_pauses: natural pauses to keep (anticipation before a big moment, rest after a payoff).
9. music: consecutive sections covering the whole video, one mood each, with the pickup target.
10. chapters: signposts, with a card where a section starts.
11. title_options and thumbnail_ideas per the packaging guidance; biggest_risk.
