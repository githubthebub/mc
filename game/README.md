# 🌍 Kindred Atlas

*A creature-befriending, world-trading game where everything you learn works in real life.*

**Play it:** open `index.html` in any modern browser. No install, no build, no dependencies.

## The pitch

Wild spirit-creatures called **Kindred** live across London, the USA, Japan and India.
You can't catch them, trap them or buy them — you can only **persuade** them, using real
negotiation technique (Chris Voss's mirroring, labeling, calibrated questions; Charlie
Houpert's conviction and warmth). Befriended Kindred gather trade goods; you haggle those
goods in markets (learning anchoring, "never split the difference" and strategic silence),
and goods sell for **1.6× far from home** — so you fly the world, picking up **cultural
fluency** in each country as you go: how to queue in London, tip in America, bow in Japan,
and haggle joyfully in India.

Every technique you use is captured in your **Field Notes journal (J)** — a growing,
readable collection of real negotiation, charisma and behavioural-science ideas
(Rory Sutherland's framing, provenance and costly signaling power the game's economy).

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / arrows | Move |
| **E** | Talk / negotiate / trade |
| **M** | World map — fly anywhere in one click |
| **J** | Field Notes journal |
| **H** | Help |

## The loop

1. **Negotiate** with a Kindred → it joins you and gathers goods over time
2. **Chat with locals** → cultural fluency rises → better prices, and shy Kindred (🔒) reveal themselves at 40% fluency
3. **Haggle** your goods to the merchant → money
4. **Fly** somewhere new (foreign goods = 1.6× price) → repeat
5. Spend profits at **The Outfitter** on upgrades that compound your earnings

Befriend all 12 Kindred to become **Atlas Master**.
