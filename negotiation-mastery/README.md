# Negotiation Mastery

A complete, field-tested system for negotiating with **anyone** — business partners, employers, vendors, landlords, family — built from the work of the field's leading practitioners and researchers, with dedicated cultural playbooks for the **USA, London/UK, India, Germany, and Japan**.

## The five laws that govern everything in this guide

1. **Negotiation is discovery, not battle.** Your goal is to surface information — theirs and the situation's. The person who knows more shapes the deal. (Voss, Malhotra)
2. **He or she who has the better alternative has the power.** Leverage lives in your BATNA, not your title, your anger, or your eloquence. (Fisher & Ury)
3. **People decide emotionally and justify rationally.** Address the emotion first or the logic never lands. (Voss, Diamond)
4. **"No deal" must always be a real option for you.** The moment you *need* the deal, you've already paid for it. Never be so sure of what you want that you wouldn't take something better.
5. **Culture changes the *how*, not the *what*.** Everyone everywhere responds to empathy, fairness, and self-interest — but how trust is built, how "no" is said, and who decides varies enormously by country. Master the invariants first, then localize.

## How to actually get good

Reading makes you knowledgeable. Only reps make you dangerous. The drill regimen in [Module 8](07-scenarios-drills.md) turns every coffee order, customer-service call, and meeting into free practice. Commit to one micro-drill per day for 60 days and the techniques stop being scripts and start being reflexes.
