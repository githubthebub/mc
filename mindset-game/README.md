# Inner Game — The Mindset Gym

A tiny browser game that trains the way you respond to real life.

Each session deals you 10 everyday situations — public criticism, cancelled plans,
the 6 AM alarm, a room full of strangers — and every choice you make trains or
erodes five mindset muscles:

| Stat | Coach (inspiration) | Core idea |
|------|--------------------|-----------|
| **Grit** | Mark Manson | Choose your struggles; values over comfort |
| **Focus** | Ali Abdaal | Feel-good productivity; systems over motivation |
| **Clarity** | Dr. K (HealthyGamerGG) | Feel and name emotions instead of dodging them |
| **Joy** | Alysa Liu | Process over outcome; play protects persistence |
| **Presence** | Charisma on Command | Social courage; confidence without performance |

After each choice, the relevant coach tells you *why* it builds or erodes the
muscle. The session ends with your mindset archetype, your weakest stat, and a
concrete real-world quest to train it today. Daily streaks and your personal
best are stored locally in your browser.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build, no dependencies, no network.

```
open mindset-game/index.html
```
