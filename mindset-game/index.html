The Mindset Gym — ten rounds of real life. Train the way you respond.

Life doesn't get easier — you get better at meeting it. In each round you'll face a situation you've probably lived through this week. Every choice trains (or erodes) five mindset muscles. Your coaches will tell you exactly why. There are no perfect runs — only honest ones.
