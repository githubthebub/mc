# Privacy Policy (Placeholder)

_Last updated: [DATE — fill before release]_

## The short version

**Your data stays on your device.** Compass is designed to work entirely offline. It does not have a backend server, does not sync to the cloud, and does not transmit your reflections, journal entries, moods, or any other content anywhere.

## What we collect

**Nothing leaves your device.** All information you enter — questionnaire answers, journal entries, mood logs, thought records, quest progress, and pattern tags — is stored locally in encrypted on-device storage. We (the developers) cannot see it, because it is never sent to us or anyone else.

## Analytics and tracking

Compass ships with **no third-party analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs**. There is no tracking of your behaviour.

If an optional, opt-in diagnostic feature is ever added, it will be **off by default**, will contain **no personal content**, and will be clearly described here before it ships.

## Network access

Compass does not make network calls that contain your content. Crisis-resource information is bundled with the app as local data files.

## Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time from within the app (Settings → Erase my data), or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes the local database.

## Children

Compass is intended for adults (see the age requirement in the app). It is not directed at children.
